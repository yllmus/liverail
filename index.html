Known coverage gaps — not hidden

No country has "worst right now" ranked live, only historical reliability (Rankings tab). PL and Germany's map positions are schedule-interpolated rather than GPS and only resolve for a minority of trains. Germany's live coverage skews toward DB Regio / S-Bahn service; long-distance ICE/IC visibility is limited. Route labels for Austria, Poland, and Germany are the raw operator line codes (e.g. RE54) — no human-friendly line description exists in the source data yet.

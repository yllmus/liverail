Known coverage gaps — not hidden

No country has "worst right now" ranked live, only historical reliability (Rankings tab). PL and Germany have no real live-GPS source — their map markers are interpolated from the schedule and only resolve for a minority of trains; watch for the dashed "estimated" marker. Germany's live coverage skews toward DB Regio / S-Bahn service; long-distance ICE/IC visibility is limited. Route labels for Austria, Poland, and Germany are the raw operator line codes (e.g. RE54) — no human-friendly line description exists in the source data yet.
